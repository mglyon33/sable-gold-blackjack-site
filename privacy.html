Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

Overview

Sable & Gold Blackjack is designed to work without an account. The app does not sell personal information, display third-party advertising, or use third-party tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your virtual-chip balance, settings, completed-purchase identifiers, active round, and gameplay statistics are stored locally so the game can function and recover an interrupted round.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The app receives product and transaction information needed to verify and deliver purchases. The provider does not receive your full payment-card number or Apple Account credentials.

Data collection

The provider does not collect gameplay data or personal information from the app in version 1.0. Apple may process purchase, diagnostic, and App Store data under Apple’s own privacy terms.

Children

The app contains frequent simulated gambling and is intended only for adults. It is not directed to children.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you delete the app or clear it through device-level controls. Consumable purchases cannot be restored after local delivery and use.
