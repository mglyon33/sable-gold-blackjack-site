Support

We want the table to feel clear, fair, and dependable.

Contact

Email blackjack@dailysocialai.com. Include the app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Never email payment-card details, Apple passwords, or verification codes.

Restore Sable & Gold Pro

Open the app, choose More, open Sable & Gold Pro, and tap Restore Pro purchase. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Chip packs

Chip packs are consumable and cannot be restored after delivery and use. If Apple shows a completed charge but chips were not delivered, restart the app while online. Its StoreKit transaction listener will attempt verified delivery again.

Refunds and billing

Apple processes all payments. Visit Apple Billing Support for billing questions or refund requests.

Responsible play

Sable & Gold has no real-money gambling or prizes. If simulated play stops being enjoyable, take a break, use Screen Time, disable in-app purchases, or delete the app. In the United States, call or text 1-800-GAMBLER for confidential gambling support.
